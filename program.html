PRELIMINARY PROGRAM

AI for Mathematics

From Machine Reasoning to Mathematical Discovery

CUHK-Shenzhen | 3 December 2026 | 09:30–17:30 · UTC+8

AI systems are making rapid progress in mathematical reasoning and are beginning to contribute to research-level problems. This meeting brings together mathematicians, AI developers, and researchers using AI in their daily work.

The morning will focus on how mathematical reasoning systems are developed and verified. The afternoon will explore what happens when these systems are used to investigate open mathematical and physical problems.
