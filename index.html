Conference Themes

1. AI for Mathematical Reasoning

  • Overview of leading AI models and geopolitics of LLMs
  • Building mathematical reasoning agents and harnesses: methods, tools, and evaluation.
  • Current limitations, research challenges, and what AI developers need from mathematicians.

2. Formalizing Mathematics with Lean

  • State of the art in AI-assisted formalization and theorem proving.
  • Future integration of Lean into mathematical research: Opportunities and prospects.
  • Combining Lean with LLMs: training methods, and challenges.

3. Mathematics in the Age of AI

  • New practices of individual and collective mathematical research.
  • New opportunities for discovery and research in maths and physics.
  • The role of mathematicians today
