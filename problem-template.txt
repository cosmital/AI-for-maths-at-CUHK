AI FOR MATHEMATICS AT CUHK-SHENZHEN
Problem preparation template

This template helps you prepare a contribution. It does not submit anything
or register you for the December event.

1. Problem title and mathematical area

2. Your name and affiliation

3. Precise statement
   Define notation, assumptions, and the desired conclusion.

4. Motivation and context
   Why does the question matter? What related results are known?

5. Current status
   Open question / partial result / conjecture / computational question.
   Include references and say which aspects you have checked.

6. Previous approaches
   Mathematical methods, computations, examples, and AI-assisted attempts.

7. Verification
   What would count as a solution or useful progress?
   How could another researcher independently check the result?

8. AI experiments, if any
   Record tools/models, relevant prompts or code, time/computational effort,
   results, and failure cases that others could learn from.

9. Sharing
   Indicate what you are willing to make public and acknowledge collaborators.
