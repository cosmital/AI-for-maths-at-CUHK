ABOUT THE MEETING

About the conference

A proposed one-day conference at CUHK-Shenzhen on AI for mathematical reasoning, proof, and discovery.

Scientific scope

The preliminary morning program covers the development and verification of AI systems for mathematical reasoning. The afternoon covers AI research agents, mathematical discovery, mathematics and physics collaborations, and research case studies.

We are interested in the whole research process: finding a useful question, experimenting with ideas, developing an argument, checking the result, and understanding where an approach fails.

The December edition is being developed as a place for mathematicians, AI researchers, and students to compare perspectives and begin new conversations.
