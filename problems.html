PREPARE A SHORT PROBLEM PITCH

Start with the mathematics.

A compact, precise description helps participants understand the question and assess possible approaches.

01

A precise statement

Give the assumptions, notation, and exact conclusion you are seeking.

02

Why it matters

Explain the context, known results, and what makes the question interesting.

03

What you have tried

Include partial progress, examples, computations, and any previous AI-assisted attempts.

04

How to check progress

Describe what a proof, counterexample, verified computation, or useful intermediate result would look like.

A short template is included to help you prepare.

PROPOSED AFTERNOON SESSION

A problem.
A few minutes.
A new perspective.

We propose short problem introductions followed by a working discussion. The aim is to clarify useful next experiments, compare possible approaches, and identify people who would like to continue the work.

Presentation length, selection, and submission arrangements will be announced with the final program.

THINKING BEYOND A SINGLE DAY

Make results useful to others.

When sharing an AI-assisted attempt, consider including the model or tool, the important steps, the verification method, and the computational effort. Clear records help the community learn from both progress and failure.
